What Is Glutathione and Why Does It Matter?

Glutathione is a naturally occurring tripeptide made up of three amino acids: cysteine, glutamate, and glycine. Found in virtually every cell of the human body, it serves as a powerful antioxidant that:

  • Neutralizes free radicals — unstable molecules that can damage cells and accelerate aging
  • Supports liver detoxification — helping the body process and eliminate toxins
  • Boosts immune cell function — particularly important for T-cell production
  • Protects mitochondria — the energy powerhouses of cells
  • Helps recycle other antioxidants — including vitamins C and E

As we age, stress accumulates, and environmental toxins build up, our natural glutathione levels often decline. Studies suggest that low glutathione levels are associated with increased oxidative stress and faster cellular aging.
