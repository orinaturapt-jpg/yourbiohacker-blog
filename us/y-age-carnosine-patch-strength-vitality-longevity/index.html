What Is Carnosine and Why Does It Matter?

Carnosine is a naturally occurring dipeptide found primarily in muscle tissue and the brain. As we age, carnosine levels decline significantly — and this drop is associated with reduced muscle performance, slower recovery times, and increased susceptibility to oxidative stress.

  • Support brain health and cognitive function
  • Contribute to healthy aging at the cellular level
