Photobiomodulation: How Light Therapy Is Changing Wellness

For centuries, humans have recognized the healing potential of light. Today, photobiomodulation (PBM) is at the forefront of wellness innovation — reshaping how we think about energy, recovery, and vitality.

What Is Photobiomodulation?

Photobiomodulation, sometimes referred to as low-level light therapy (LLLT), uses specific wavelengths of light to interact with biological tissue. Studies suggest targeted light wavelengths — particularly in the red and near-infrared spectrum — may support:

  • Cellular energy production through enhanced mitochondrial activity
  • Skin health and rejuvenation by promoting collagen synthesis
  • Recovery from physical exertion
  • Relaxation and stress management

Unlike invasive procedures, photobiomodulation is non-invasive and non-transdermal.

The Science Behind Light and the Body

Photobiomodulation delivers photons to cellular photoreceptors — most notably cytochrome c oxidase in the mitochondria. When these photoreceptors absorb light energy, they may enhance ATP production, the primary energy currency of cells.
