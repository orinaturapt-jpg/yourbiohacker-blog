What Is GHK-Cu and Why Does It Matter?

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring copper complex composed of the tripeptide glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine bound to a copper ion. Studies suggest it plays a critical role in:

  • Skin regeneration — supporting collagen and elastin production
  • Anti-inflammatory responses — helping the body modulate oxidative stress
  • DNA repair — may support the body's natural gene expression related to cellular health
  • Wound healing — long studied for its role in tissue repair and remodeling

Research published in journals including Biomolecules and the Journal of Aging Research highlights GHK-Cu's remarkable ability to influence over 4,000 human genes — many associated with age-related decline.
