What Is Stem Cell Activation?

Stem cells are the body's master repair cells. They are undifferentiated, meaning they have the potential to become many different cell types — from muscle and bone to skin and nerve tissue. Stem cell activation refers to the process of stimulating dormant or sluggish stem cells back into action. Rather than introducing foreign cells into the body, activation science focuses on waking up the stem cells already present in your tissues.
